About

KIMS Ecity — Anaesthesia Case Registry, v4.0

A single-page web application for the Department of Anaesthesia, KIMS Hospital, Electronic City, Bengaluru.

HOD: Dr. Subramanyam Mahankali HOD

Department Lead: Dr. Vivek Rayadurg Lead

What’s new in v4

  • OT & Out-of-OT logs — one toggle on the entry form, three dashboard views.
  • Anaesthetist 1, 2, 3 — cases credited to every named anaesthetist.
  • 2nd anaesthetist workload chart — visibility of teaching / joint cases.
  • 24-hour shift summary table on every dashboard view (08:00 rollover).
  • Dual techniques — Epidural+GA and Spinal analgesia+GA captured explicitly.
  • Intrathecal opioid audit — morphine and fentanyl dosages tracked.
  • 10-item comorbidity checklist.
  • PACU pathway in post-op disposition.

Data is stored in a private departmental Google Sheet. Accessible only to authorised members of the department.

A companion registry exists for KIMS Mahadevapura. The two are independent — separate sheets, separate doctor lists, separate dashboards.
