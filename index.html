Definitions. WIT1 — living donor: renal artery clamp to cold flush; DCD: functional warm ischaemia (sustained SBP < 50 mmHg or SpO₂ < 70% to cold perfusion); DBD: not applicable. CIT — start of cold perfusion/flush to kidney out of ice. Anastomosis time (WIT2) — kidney out of ice to reperfusion. DGF — dialysis within 7 days. SGF — no dialysis, creatinine > 3.0 mg/dL on day 5 (days 4–6 accepted). IGF — no dialysis, day-5 creatinine ≤ 3.0 mg/dL. Day-1 / day-2 urine — the 24-hour volumes entered on post-op days 1 and 2 in Graft F/U.
